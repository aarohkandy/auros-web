The prices are on the page because a price you have to ask for is a price that depends on how
desperate you sound.

## What you are actually paying for

Not the operating system. Fedora is free, Universal Blue is free, and the applications come from
Flathub and are free. If those three things were the product we would have nothing to sell you
and you should not pay us.

What you are paying for is that somebody watches the nightly build, that an image only reaches
your machines after it has booted in a virtual machine and passed a fixed matrix of checks, that
the removal list is asserted every single night so upstream cannot quietly put back the things
you asked us to take out, and that when a serious vulnerability lands there is one file to
change and your machines have it by the next restart.

You are paying for the maintenance and the evidence. The image is the artefact.

## About the comparison to buying new

Our real competitor is doing nothing. Doing nothing is free, and it is what most schools in this
position will choose, and any pricing page that pretends the alternative is a purchase order for
new laptops is arguing with a strawman.

So the school tier carries the arithmetic rather than a saving. We are not going to print a
figure. We have no customers, so any figure we printed would be invented, and a number you
cannot check is worth less than no number at all.

## What is not priced yet

The installation media. The Windows-side program deliberately does not write your USB stick —
raw writes to a disk on a machine we do not own, with no undo, is the single most destructive
thing this software could do — so the stick is made once, elsewhere, and then does every
machine. Who makes it is an open question. Either we publish a downloadable image with every
build, or you produce one from the published image yourself.

Today only the second is written down, it lives on the replaceable page, and it needs a command
line. By our own rule that means it is not yet part of what these prices buy. Nobody here has
decided which way it goes or what it costs, so rather than leave you to work it out from the
exclusions list, here it is: ask before you order, and if it has not been decided, that is the
answer you will get.
